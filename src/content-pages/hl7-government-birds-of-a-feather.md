# HL7 Government Birds of a Feather

**Wednesday, September 23, 2026 9:00 AM-10:30 AM  EST**

The Federal Electronic Health Record Modernization (FEHRM) office hosts the only Government affinity group meeting (“Birds of a Feather”) during HL7 Working Group meetings, which take place three times annually. This is the only open forum that brings together government and industry members to discuss interoperability standards, policies, and best practices to enhance collaboration across government departments and agencies.

Speakers from CMS, ONC, industry, and the FEHRM will share their updates on policy, adoption, and progress.
