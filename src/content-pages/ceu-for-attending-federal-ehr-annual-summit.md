## Get Continuing Education Units for Attending the 2026 Federal EHR Annual Summit

You need to claim credit only once for the total number of educational sessions you participated in (up to 7.5 credits) at the end of the day. 

* For psychologists claiming APA credit visit <https://cme.partnersed.com/APAEHR26>
* For all other participants (not psychologists) visit <https://partnersed.com/EHR26>

 You will need to:

1. Sign up or log in to the applicable site.
2. Complete the activity evaluation by rating each session you attended.
3. Upon completion of all evaluation questions, your credit will be made available for download immediately.
